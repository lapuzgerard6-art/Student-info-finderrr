How To Use This System

1. Create an Account

Register with your name, email, password, and date of birth before logging in.

2. Log In

Use the email and password you registered with to access the system.

3. Add Students

Go to Student Finder and fill in the "Add a Student" form to build your database.

4. Search

Type a name, ID, course, or section into the search bar to instantly filter results.

5. View Details

Click "View Details" on any student card to see their full information in a popup.

6. Leave Feedback

Rate the system and leave a comment on the Feedback page to help us improve.
